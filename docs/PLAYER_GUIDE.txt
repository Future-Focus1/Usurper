USURPER — THE CROWN IS EMPTY

Extract the entire archive, then run Usurper.exe.

Choose one of four claimants. Each week, review your position, answer the
petitions, issue one decree, and read the realm's response. The Map, Court,
Ledger, Army and World screens do not spend a decree.

Read the forecast before recruiting. Gold pays troops; rations feed them.
Protect witnesses, keep promises, and find evidence inside this campaign.
Support and alliances can turn an estate into part of your economy.

Claim 100 permits an attempt to settle the succession. Each throne route
also has its own conditions. Common routes show their missing requirements;
unusual settlements appear when discovered. A failed attempt ends the claim.
The succession is finite: a Council can settle a campaign that remains open.

In battle, choose a lane and an order. Commit reserves to reinforce weak
positions. You can move companies before the opening clash. The seven orders
have different risks; tooltips explain them. Retreat can preserve your host.

Press Esc during a campaign to pause and save. There is an autosave and three
manual slots. Continue resumes the latest save, including a pending battle
or a completed epilogue. Fullscreen is available under Settings.

Saves, Chronicle and preferences live in:
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Usurper\

The Chronicle remembers completed campaigns and discovered stories. A new
claimant must still uncover the truth personally. The public World atlas
contains the history and geography everyone in Nezrel could know.

Settings includes volume sliders and mute switches for master audio,
background music, menu tone and menu clicks. Audio preferences are saved.
The game includes textual political violence, bereavement,
disputed assault allegations, coercion, and end-of-life mercy.

Godot and font dependency notices are included beside the executable.
